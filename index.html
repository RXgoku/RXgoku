The Barako Story

Strong name.
Stronger brews.

"Barako" means bold — and that's the only way we know how to brew. What started as one tiny counter and a shaker has grown into Mumbai's favourite boba ritual: premium teas steeped fresh every few hours, pearls cooked in small batches, and syrups we caramelise ourselves.

From classic milk teas to refreshing fruit concoctions and unique specialty blends — plus steamed baos and momos for the snackers — every cup is an answer to the mundane.
