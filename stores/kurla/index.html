About this room

Phoenix Marketcity in Kurla is one of the largest malls in the country, and a mall day is long, loud and hard on the feet. This is the Barako to sit down in halfway through. It is inside the mall, so expect its opening and closing to follow the mall's, and check the store page for the confirmed hours before making it the plan.

Like every Barako, it does the whole menu: handcrafted boba, ceremonial Japanese matcha whisked to order, coffee, the live ramen bar, and a steamer of vegetarian bao, momos and dumplings. Sit as long as you like.

On the menu

  • Milk teasBrown sugar, taro, classic. Pearls cooked through the day.
  • Fruit teas and sodasLychee, butterfly pea, strawberry. Cold, sharp, fizzy if you want.
  • Ceremonial matchaJapanese ceremonial grade, whisked to order. Hot, iced, or as a latte.
  • CoffeeIt is in the name. Espresso drinks, hot and cold.
  • Ramen barA Nongshim pack, cooked live. Egg, cheese, greens, chicken, chilli oil. Your call.
  • Bao and dim sumBao, momos, dumplings. Steamed, and every one of them vegetarian.
